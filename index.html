1. Registration and Access

        
            Q1. I didn't register. Can I still attend?             
                

For Media Representatives:

                
                        
  • If the request is received within two weeks of the event, we will be unable to accommodate the media representative.
    •                     
  • If the request is received more than two weeks before the event, the guest should be directed to the Media Team for registration and further assistance.
    •                 
                

Pending confirmation: Media Team contact details and email address to be provided by Khadija or Eman ElShenawi.

                

For General Attendees:

                

Yes, you can register through the official Dubai Future Week registration platform. Once registration is completed successfully, you will receive a confirmation email and QR code for entry.

            
        
        
            Q2. The session I want to attend is fully booked. Can an exception be made?             
                

If a session is fully booked, no additional seats can be accommodated. However, please keep an eye on the Dubai Future Week app, as any cancellations will be reflected in the app and the seats will become available for registration.

            
        
        
            Q3. Can I transfer my registration to someone else?             
                

No. Registrations are non-transferable and can only be used by the person whose name is registered.

            
        
        
            Q4. I registered but did not receive a confirmation email. What should I do?             
                

Please check your junk or spam folder first. If the confirmation email is still missing, complete the registration process again and ensure that you enter the correct email address. If the issue persists, you can reach out to the concerned team here.

            
        
        
            Q5. My QR code is not working. What should I do?             
                

If you experience any issues with the QR code, please visit the help desk for assistance. Our team will be happy to troubleshoot the issue and provide the necessary support to resolve it as quickly as possible.

            
        
        
            Q6. Can I access multiple venues with one registration?             
                

Yes. Dubai Future Week registration provides access to the event venues, subject to the access requirements for specific sessions and areas.

            
        
        
            Q7. Will on-site registration be available?             
                

Yes, on-site registration is available. However, we recommend registering online in advance for your convenience. This will help you save time, avoid potential queues upon arrival, and ensure a smoother and more enjoyable event experience.

            
        
        
            Q8. Can I attend any session?             
                

Yes, most sessions are open to the general public. Sessions with restricted access will be marked as “By Invitation Only.”

3. Event Information and Facilities

        
            Q12. What are the event dates and timings?             
                

Dubai Future Week will take place from 18 to 21 November, from 9:00 AM to 10:00 PM daily, except for the final day (21 November), when the event will conclude at 8:00 PM.

Yes. Food and beverage options will be available as follows:

                
                        
  • General Attendees: Restaurants located within the District.
    •                     
  • Media Representatives: Catering will be available in the Media Lounge.

            Q15. Can I take photos or videos during the event?             
                

Yes. Photography and videography are permitted during the event.

            
        
        
            Q16. Will there be security screening?             
                

Yes. All attendees will be subject to security screening before entering the event venues.

            
        
        
            Q17. Can I visit the Museum of the Future and attend Dubai Future Week on the same day?             
                

The Museum of the Future will be closed to regular visitors during Dubai Future Week. Only the Auditorium will be accessible as part of the event.

            
        
                 

4. Badges and Registration Details

        
            Q18. I entered incorrect information during registration. How can I update my details?             
                

You may update your details directly at the registration kiosk. However, any updates or changes to the registration information must be verified and confirmed by the Help Desk.

            
        
        
            Q19. What should I do if I lose my badge or access pass?             
                

If you lose your badge or access pass, please approach the Help Desk. Our team will assist in cancelling the lost badge and issuing a replacement.

            
        
        
            Q20. Can I collect my badge in advance?             
                

Yes, you can collect your badge starting November 5 at the Registration Hub located at Emirates Towers.
